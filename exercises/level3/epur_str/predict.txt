@@ question
match: exact
args: [" this        time it      will     be    more complex  . "]
answer: this time it will be more complex .$
    $> ./epur_str " this        time it      will     be    more complex  . " | cat -e
---
Every run of blanks becomes one space, leading and trailing blanks vanish. The lone "."
is a word too.

@@ question
match: exact
args: ["\t\ta\t\tb\t"]
answer: a b$
wrong: a	b$ => The output separator is always a SPACE, whatever blanks were in the input.
    $> ./epur_str $'\t\ta\t\tb\t' | cat -e
---
Tabs are blanks: they're skipped, and the words are joined with a single space.
