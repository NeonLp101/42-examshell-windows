wrong: HelloWorld$ => ft_putstr prints no newline - nothing ends with $ here.
wrong: Hello$World$ => ft_putstr prints no newline.
What does the program output, as `cat -e` shows it?

    ft_putstr("Hello");
    ft_putstr("World");
---
ft_putstr writes exactly the characters of the string, nothing more: no newline, so
`cat -e` shows no `$` at all.

@@ question
answer: 3
How many times is `write` called by this ft_putstr?

    ft_putstr("abc");
---
One call per character: 'a', 'b', 'c'. The '\0' is never written - the loop stops on it.

@@ question
answer: 4
What is the value of this expression?

    sizeof("abc")
---
A string literal is an array of its characters PLUS the '\0': 4 bytes.
That's why `write(1, "\n", 1)` uses 1 and not sizeof.
