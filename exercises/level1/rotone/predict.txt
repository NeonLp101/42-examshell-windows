@@ question
match: exact
args: ["zZ"]
answer: aA$
wrong: {[$ => 'z' + 1 is '{' in ASCII - rotone wraps around to 'a' (and 'Z' to 'A').
    $> ./rotone "zZ" | cat -e
---
The wrap-around: 'z' -> 'a', 'Z' -> 'A'.

@@ question
match: exact
args: ["AkjhZ zLKIJz , 23y "]
answer: BlkiA aMLJKa , 23z $
wrong: BlkiA aMLJKa , 23z$ => The trailing space of the input is printed too - before the $.
    $> ./rotone "AkjhZ zLKIJz , 23y " | cat -e
---
Digits, commas and spaces are unchanged - including the space at the end, which `cat -e`
makes visible before the `$`.
