How many bytes must `buf` have, at least, for this copy?

    ft_strcpy(buf, "hello world");
---
"hello world" is 11 characters, and strcpy also copies the '\0': 12 bytes. strcpy never
checks the size of the destination - a too small buffer silently overflows.
