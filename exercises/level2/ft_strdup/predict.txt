@@ question
answer: 8
wrong: 6 => sizeof of a POINTER doesn't care about the string: it's the size of the address.
On a 64-bit machine, what does this print?

    char *src = "hello";
    printf("%d", (int)sizeof(src));
---
src is a char * - sizeof gives the size of the pointer itself (8 bytes on 64-bit), not
the length of the string. Use a length loop (or strlen) instead.
