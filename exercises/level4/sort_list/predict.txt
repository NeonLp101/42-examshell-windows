@@ question
answer: 3
How many swaps does sort_list make? (cmp = ascending)

    sort_list([3, 2, 1], ascending)
---
Each swap of two neighbours fixes exactly one pair that's in the wrong order.
[3, 2, 1] has three: (3,2), (3,1), (2,1) => 3 swaps, whatever the walking strategy.
